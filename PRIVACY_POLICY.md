# Privacy Policy for Audion PSAP

**Last Updated: November 20, 2025**

## Introduction

Audion PSAP ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Personal Sound Amplification Product (PSAP) application.

## Information We Collect

### Audio Data
Audion PSAP uses your device's microphone for the following purposes:
- **Hearing Tests**: To conduct pure tone audiometry tests that assess your hearing sensitivity at different frequencies
- **Calibration**: To determine your comfortable and uncomfortable listening levels
- **Audio Processing**: To provide real-time sound amplification based on your personalized hearing profile

### Local Storage
The app stores the following data locally on your device:
- Hearing test results (audiogram data)
- Calibration profile settings (MCL/UCL levels)
- User-created hearing profiles
- Preferences and settings

## How We Use Your Information

All audio processing and data storage occurs **entirely on your device**. We use this information to:
- Generate personalized hearing profiles
- Apply real-time audio enhancement based on your hearing needs
- Save and manage multiple user profiles
- Provide you with visual representations of your hearing test results

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

**We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal information or audio data with third parties.** All data remains on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.

## Data Security

- All hearing test results and profiles are stored locally using encrypted database storage
- Audio captured during tests is processed in real-time and is not permanently recorded or saved
- No audio recordings are created or stored during normal operation
- Your data remains on your device and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app

## Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

### Required Permissions
- **RECORD_AUDIO**: Essential for conducting hearing tests and providing real-time audio amplification
- **MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS**: Required to adjust audio output for personalized hearing enhancement

These permissions are used solely for the core functionality of the app and cannot be disabled without affecting app performance.

## Children's Privacy

Audion PSAP is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access all data stored by the app through the app interface
- Delete your hearing profiles at any time within the app
- Remove all app data by uninstalling the application

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Medical Disclaimer

Audion PSAP is a Personal Sound Amplification Product intended for non-hearing impaired consumers to amplify environmental sound for recreational use. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you believe you have hearing loss, please consult a licensed hearing healthcare professional.
