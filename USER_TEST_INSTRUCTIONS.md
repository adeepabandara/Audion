# How to Take Your Hearing Test

## Before You Start

### What You'll Need:
- ✅ **Quiet environment** - Find a silent room with minimal background noise
- ✅ **Headphones** - Use good quality headphones (over-ear or in-ear)
- ✅ **5-7 minutes** - The test takes about 3-5 minutes per ear
- ✅ **Full attention** - Focus only on the test, avoid distractions

### Important Tips:
- 🔇 **Turn off** TV, music, fans, and close windows
- 📱 **Silence** your phone (except this app)
- 👥 **Tell others** you need quiet for a few minutes
- 🪑 **Sit comfortably** in a quiet spot
- 🎧 **Wear headphones correctly** - check Left/Right labels

---

## How the Test Works

### What You'll Hear:
- **Beeping tones** at different pitches (low to high)
- Each tone lasts about **1.5 seconds**
- **Some tones will be very soft** - that's normal!
- **Some tones you might not hear** - that's okay too!

### What to Do:
1. **Listen carefully** for any beeping sound
2. **Tap the circle** IMMEDIATELY when you hear a tone
3. **Don't tap** if you don't hear anything
4. **Wait** for the next tone (2-4 seconds between tones)

### Important Rules:
- ❌ **Don't guess** - Only tap if you actually hear the tone
- ❌ **Don't tap randomly** - Wait to hear a sound first
- ✅ **Be consistent** - If you hear it, tap it!
- ✅ **Stay focused** - The whole test takes about 3 minutes per ear

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Put On Your Headphones
```
🎧 Make sure:
   - Left headphone on left ear
   - Right headphone on right ear  
   - Comfortable fit (not too tight)
   - No hair blocking the speakers
```

### Step 2: Adjust Volume
```
📢 Set your device volume to:
   - About 70-80% maximum
   - Comfortable listening level
   - Not too loud, not too quiet
```

### Step 3: Start with Right Ear
```
👂 The test will automatically start with your RIGHT ear
   - You'll see "Right Ear – Pure Tone Test" on screen
   - Get ready to listen carefully
```

### Step 4: Listen and Tap
```
👂 For each tone:
   
   1. LISTEN 🎵
      ↓
   2. Heard it? → TAP THE CIRCLE ✓
      ↓
   3. Didn't hear? → Wait for next tone
      ↓
   4. REPEAT for all tones
```

### Step 5: The Pattern You'll Experience

**First Phase (Going Down):**
```
Tone 1: BEEP (loud) → Tap ✓
Tone 2: BEEP (softer) → Tap ✓
Tone 3: BEEP (very soft) → Tap ✓
Tone 4: beep (too soft) → Don't tap
```

**Second Phase (Going Up):**
```
Tone 5: beep (soft) → Don't hear → Wait
Tone 6: BEEP (little louder) → Heard it! → Tap ✓
Tone 7: beep (soft again) → Don't hear → Wait
Tone 8: BEEP (little louder) → Heard it! → Tap ✓

✅ Frequency complete! Moving to next pitch...
```

### Step 6: Progress Through All Frequencies
```
The test will automatically move through 7 different pitches:
   1. Medium tone (1000 Hz)
   2. High tone (2000 Hz)
   3. Very high tone (4000 Hz)
   4. Extremely high tone (8000 Hz)
   5. Low tone (500 Hz)
   6. Very low tone (250 Hz)
   7. Medium tone again (1000 Hz - retest)

Progress bar shows: ▓▓▓▓▓░░ (5/7 done)
```

### Step 7: Complete Left Ear
```
After right ear finishes:
   - You'll see "Left Ear – Pure Tone Test"
   - Repeat the same process
   - Listen and tap for 3-5 minutes
```

### Step 8: Review Your Results
```
After both ears:
   - You'll see your audiogram (hearing chart)
   - Lower points = better hearing
   - Higher points = more hearing loss
```

## What to Expect

### Typical Experience:

**First 3-5 tones:**
- Usually EASY to hear
- You'll tap several times
- Tones get quieter each time

**Middle tones:**
- Might not hear 2-3 tones
- This is NORMAL - don't worry!
- Tones are very soft on purpose

**Last 3-5 tones:**
- Tones get slightly louder again
- You'll hear and tap about 2-4 times
- Then it moves to the next pitch

### How Many Times to Tap:
- **Minimum:** 2 taps per frequency (if threshold is at starting level)
- **Typical:** 3-5 taps per frequency (normal hearing)
- **Maximum:** 6-10 taps per frequency (difficult threshold)

**Total taps for complete test: ~25-35 taps per ear**

## Common Questions

### "I'm not hearing anything!"
- ✅ **Normal!** Some tones are meant to be too soft to hear
- ✅ **Don't tap** if you don't hear anything
- ✅ **Wait patiently** for the next tone (2-4 seconds)
- ✅ If you don't hear ANY tones, check your headphones and volume

### "Should I tap if I'm not sure?"
- ❌ **No** - Only tap if you CLEARLY hear a beep
- ❌ **Don't guess** - Guessing makes results inaccurate
- ✅ **Be honest** - It's okay to not hear soft tones

### "How loud should the tones be?"
- 🔊 **Varies!** Some will be loud, some very soft
- 🔊 **First tones:** Usually easy to hear (comfortable volume)
- 🔊 **Middle tones:** Might be very soft or silent
- 🔊 **Last tones:** Slightly louder again

### "Can I take a break?"
- ⏸️ **Not during a frequency** - Complete the current pitch first
- ⏸️ **Between ears:** Yes! Rest for a minute before left ear
- ⏸️ **Better to complete in one sitting** - Takes only 6-10 minutes total

### "What if I accidentally tap?"
- 🤔 **One mistake is okay** - The test accounts for this
- 🤔 **Multiple mistakes** - Results might be less accurate
- 🤔 **Can restart** - If you make many errors, start over

### "The test seems long!"
- ⏱️ **Each frequency:** 25-40 seconds (normal)
- ⏱️ **All 7 frequencies:** 3-5 minutes per ear
- ⏱️ **Both ears:** 6-10 minutes total
- ⏱️ **Optimized:** Already 30% faster than clinical tests!

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Button not responding"
**Solution:** 
- Button only works DURING the tone (1.5 seconds)
- Button disabled during waiting period (2-4 seconds)
- Only one tap per tone is counted

### Problem: "Test stuck / not progressing"
**Solution:**
- Need 2 taps during "ascending" phase to advance
- Taps during "descending" phase don't count toward completion
- Maximum 30 tones per frequency (auto-advances)

### Problem: "Can't hear even loud tones"
**Solution:**
- Check headphone connection
- Increase device volume to 70-80%
- Verify headphones are working (play music)
- Make sure headphones are on correct ears (L/R)

### Problem: "Hearing tones that aren't there"
**Solution:**
- Check for background noise (AC, fan, traffic)
- Wait until room is completely quiet
- Might be tinnitus (ringing in ears) - only tap for actual test tones

## Pro Tips for Accurate Results

### 🎯 **Best Practices:**

1. **Take test in morning** - Ears are most rested, less fatigue
2. **Avoid loud noise before test** - No concerts, loud music for 24 hours
3. **Remove earwax** - Clean ears day before (not right before!)
4. **Stay hydrated** - Drink water, avoid alcohol
5. **Test multiple times** - Take test 2-3 times for consistency
6. **Same environment** - Use same room and headphones each time

### 🚫 **Avoid:**
- Testing after loud noise exposure (concerts, construction)
- Testing when tired or sick
- Testing with ear infection or cold
- Testing while stressed or distracted
- Using different headphones each test
- Testing in different noise environments
